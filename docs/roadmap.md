# Roadmap

**Status as of September 2026.** High-level direction, kept as a real snapshot rather than an
aspiration. States are kept distinct on purpose — *in production*, *in development*, *prototype*,
and *planned* are not blurred, and nothing planned is described as delivered.

## Now (in production)

- Static SPA atlas with search and honest handling of absent results
- Structured, version-controlled JSON content
- Leaflet maps with historical routes
- Per-claim certainty and provenance model (grade + nature-of-source + dating, as distinct axes)
- Entity pages as a cross-collection knowledge network, with structured metadata
- SEO layer for citable entity pages
- Controlled deployment with served-vs-built verification

## In development

- Broader coverage of verified content and collections
- Deeper provenance — claims linked to the exact folio / document location
- Digital-humanities interoperability track (see below)

## Prototype (lab, not integrated)

- Three.js 3D globe as an exploration surface
- Immersive journey reader (narrative + route)

These are evaluated for quality independently of the separate question of whether and how to
integrate them into the product.

## Planned

- Integrated temporal 3D exploration ("time machine") once an integration decision is made
- Expanded datasets
- Institutional integrations
- Education use cases

## Interoperability track (planned / designed-toward)

The data model is designed with interoperability in mind toward:

- **Linked Places Format (LPF)** — for places and their attestations
- **CIDOC-CRM** — for events, actors, and relationships
- **IIIF** — for image/document presentation and citation

This is an **architectural intention and a planned credential track**, pursued in parallel with
product work. It is stated as direction, not as a shipped feature.

## What is intentionally *not* on this public roadmap

Commercial strategy, pricing, partnership specifics, and internal prioritisation detail are
confidential and excluded from this public case study.
